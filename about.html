Our Story

Mesquite Data was founded in Austin with a straightforward mission: help Texas businesses build the digital infrastructure they need to compete and grow. We saw too many good companies held back by outdated websites, manual processes, and technical debt.

We started with a simple belief, that small and medium-sized businesses deserve the same quality of web and data solutions that enterprise companies get, without the enterprise price tag or complexity. Our clients are contractors, service companies, professional firms, and local businesses that are scaling up and need reliable technology partners.

Today, we operate from two Texas offices and work with businesses across the state. We're not trying to be the biggest agency in Texas. We're focused on being the most reliable, delivering work that's built right and actually helps our clients grow.

What Drives Us

Quality Over Speed

We don't rush projects. We build systems that work correctly, perform well, and last. Our clients appreciate that we take the time to do things right instead of cutting corners to hit arbitrary deadlines.

Clear Communication

We explain what we're building, why we recommend certain approaches, and what to expect. You always know where your project stands and what's happening next.

Long-Term Partnerships

We're not looking for one time transactions. We build relationships with businesses that plan to grow, and we stay with them as their needs evolve. Many of our clients have been with us for years.

Technical Excellence

We keep up with modern technology and best practices so our clients don't have to. Clean code, secure systems, fast performance, it's how we build everything.

Our Team

We're a team of developers, designers, and data specialists who genuinely care about the work we deliver. Everyone on our team has real-world experience building systems for growing businesses.

Every project gets attention from senior team members, not just junior developers learning on your dime.

How We Work

We're direct and practical. When you work with us, you get straight answers about what's possible, realistic timelines, and honest recommendations about the best path forward for your specific situation.

We don't oversell or push services you don't need. If something isn't worth the investment for your business, we'll tell you. If a simpler solution will work, we recommend it. Our job is to help you make smart decisions about technology, not maximize our billable hours.

We also don't disappear after launch. Whether it's a quick question, an unexpected issue, or planning the next phase of growth, we're available and responsive. That's part of what being a partner means.

Why Texas Matters

Being based in Texas isn't just geography, it's about understanding the businesses we serve. We know what it's like to operate in this market. We understand the pace of business here, the expectations, and the practical realities of running a growing company in Texas.

Our two offices in Austin and Tyler mean we're accessible. If you want to meet in person, we can do that. If you prefer remote communication, that works too. We're flexible because we're local, and we're invested in the success of Texas businesses.
